## Academic Profile

I am an interdisciplinary researcher with training in energy economics, system modeling, and data-driven sustainability analysis. My academic interests lie at the intersection of policy-oriented energy and emission modeling and applied analytical tools for industrial and sectoral decision-making. Through both academic research and independent applied projects, I aim to translate analytical and modeling approaches into decision-relevant insights, with a particular focus on emerging economies such as Vietnam.

Research experience
======
### System-level Energy & Emission Modeling
- Developed bottom-up, LEAP-based system models for Vietnam’s construction and industrial sectors.  
- Designed baseline and mitigation scenarios aligned with national commitments such as Net Zero 2050 and Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs).  
- Applied IPCC-consistent emission accounting methods and scenario analysis to evaluate technology transition and energy-efficiency pathways.  
- Produced policy-relevant insights to support long-term mitigation planning and system-level decision-making.

### Electricity Demand Forecasting & Energy Trend Analysis
- Conducted statistical and regression-based analysis to examine long-term electricity demand trajectories in Vietnam.  
- Identified socio-economic and structural drivers shaping sectoral electricity demand.  
- Contributed analytical inputs to broader energy planning and transition studies, including research conducted under international cooperation frameworks.


Professional experience
======
