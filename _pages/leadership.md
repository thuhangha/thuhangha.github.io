## Leadership Philosophy

I did not begin my academic journey as a natural leader. Coming from a science-focused and introverted background in high school, I entered university with strong analytical skills but limited confidence in collective engagement. Throughout my undergraduate years, I deliberately stepped beyond this comfort zone. Through sustained involvement in student governance and service within the School of Economics and Management at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, I gradually developed leadership as a practice of responsibility, coordination, and service to a broader academic community.

## Formal Leadership Roles (2023–2024)

During the academic year 2023–2024, I concurrently held multiple leadership roles within the Youth Union and Student Union of the School of Economics and Management, Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST). In this integrated governance structure, I was primarily responsible for financial planning, logistics coordination, and operational support across academic and student development activities.

- **Head of Finance & Logistics** – Youth Union & Student Union, School of Economics and Management, HUST  
- **Vice President** – Student Union, School of Economics and Management, HUST  
- **Executive Committee Member** – Youth Union, School of Economics and Management, HUST  

Across these roles, I supported and coordinated activities involving approximately **100 active members**, including executive officers, collaborators, and event-based volunteers. This experience strengthened my capacity to manage limited resources, align diverse stakeholders, and ensure the smooth execution of large-scale academic and student-oriented initiatives.
## Signature Leadership & Service Initiatives

Alongside formal roles, I led and contributed to a number of initiatives that reflect both leadership responsibility and academic-community engagement.

### Competitive Debate & Representation
- **Team Leader – SVBK 2023 Debate Competition (HUST)**  
  Led the debate team representing the School of Economics and Management. Our team achieved **Top 3 overall** at the university level.  
  This experience expanded my inter-university network and directly led to my invitation to join the *Meeting with PM* debate competition at the University of Economics and Business (VNU), where our team also placed **Top 3 overall**.

### Academic & Research Culture Development
- **Organizing Committee Member** – Student Scientific Research Conference (41st edition), School of Economics and Management  
- **Leader / Organizer** – Research orientation workshops and topic-selection training sessions for undergraduate students  

These initiatives aimed to strengthen research awareness, academic skills, and early exposure to scientific inquiry among students.

### Student Capacity Building & Civic Engagement
- **Lead Organizer** – Freshman Welcome Program (2023–2024)  
- **Lead Organizer** – Mid-program Civic Engagement Sessions  
- **Lead Organizer** – Freshman Camp K68, School of Economics and Management  

In these programs, I oversaw human resource coordination, logistics planning, and program execution, supporting student integration, soft-skill development, and community cohesion within the school.
## Broader Service & Community Engagement

In addition to the initiatives above, I actively supported a wide range of student-oriented activities, including:
- Civic education and student orientation programs  
- Academic workshops on CV preparation, interviews, and research skills  
- University-wide conferences, competitions, and student governance events  
- Campus engagement activities related to sports, wellbeing, and community awareness  

These experiences reinforced my understanding of leadership as sustained service rather than individual visibility.
## Honors & Recognition

My contributions to student leadership and service have been formally recognized through multiple awards and certifications, including:

- Outstanding Ambassador – Hanoi Mathematical Modeling (2021), awarded by Hanoi Mathematical Modeling & the Vietnam Association for Applied Mathematics  
- Certificate of Merit – Youth Union, Hanoi University of Science and Technology (2022–2023; 2023–2024)  
- Certificate of Merit – Rector of Hanoi University of Science and Technology for academic excellence and contributions to Youth Union and Student Union activities (2022–2023; 2023–2024)  
- Certificate of Excellence – Key Student Union Officer Training Program (2022–2023)  
- *Excellent Student of Five Merits* Award – HUST (2021–2022)
