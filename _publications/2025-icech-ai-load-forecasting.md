## Abstract
Electricity load forecasting plays a critical role in power system planning and operation, and artificial intelligence (AI) techniques have increasingly been applied to enhance forecasting accuracy. This study conducts a bibliometric analysis of research on basic AI models for electricity load forecasting using data retrieved from the Scopus database. By analyzing publication trends, leading journals, influential authors, countries, and commonly adopted AI methodologies, the paper provides a systematic overview of the evolution and current landscape of AI-based load forecasting research. The findings highlight dominant research directions, methodological preferences, and existing gaps, thereby offering insights for future studies and supporting researchers and policymakers in understanding the development of data-driven approaches in the energy sector.
