Abstract:> Industrial parks (IPs) in Vietnam contribute significantly to national GHG emissions due to reliance on grid electricity from fossil fuels. This study assesses the technical and economic feasibility of integrating rooftop PV with BESS at an industrial park in Binh Phuoc. Using HOMER Pro, two scenarios are compared (PV-only vs PV–BESS) with NPC, LCoE, and payback period. Results show PV-only is currently more economical, while sensitivity analysis indicates PV–BESS becomes more competitive under higher tariffs and lower technology costs, highlighting the role of policy support and incentives for low-carbon industrial development.
