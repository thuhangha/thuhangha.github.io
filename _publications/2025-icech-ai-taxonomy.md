abstract: >
  This study provides a comprehensive bibliometric review and taxonomy of artificial intelligence (AI) applications in electricity load forecasting.
  Using data extracted from the Scopus database, the paper systematically analyzes publication trends, leading countries, influential authors,
  and core journals in the field. Beyond descriptive statistics, the study proposes a structured taxonomy of AI-based load forecasting methods
  according to forecasting horizon, data granularity, and algorithmic approaches, including traditional machine learning and deep learning models.
  The findings highlight the rapid growth of AI-driven forecasting research and reveal emerging methodological shifts toward hybrid and deep learning
  architectures. This work contributes a structured knowledge map for researchers and policymakers, supporting the development of more robust
  and context-aware load forecasting models in data-driven energy systems.
