## Core Research & Applied Projects

### 1) LEAP-based Mitigation Scenario & System-level Decision Modeling  
**Type:** Academic & Policy-oriented Research Project  
**Focus:** Vietnam construction sector decarbonization; scenario design; decision support

This project develops a bottom-up, LEAP-based system model to support long-term emission-related decision-making in Vietnam’s construction sector. The model represents material and energy flows across the sector and evaluates baseline and mitigation pathways aligned with Vietnam’s Net Zero 2050 and NDC targets.

The study applies IPCC-consistent emission accounting methods and scenario analysis to assess the impacts of technology transition and energy-efficiency improvements on sectoral emissions. The results provide policy-relevant insights for mitigation planning and system-level decision support.

**Outputs & recognition:** Accepted for presentation at **EEEAM 2025 (IEEE-affiliated)**; awarded **Best Thesis (9.8/10)** for methodological rigor and scenario design.

### 2) GHG Inventory & Multi-sector Emission Accounting Tool  
**Type:** Applied Research & Implementation Project  
**Focus:** facility-level GHG inventory; MRV readiness; regulatory alignment

This project focuses on developing a systematized GHG inventory framework to support facility-level emission reporting and data standardization under Vietnam’s regulatory context. The framework translates national reporting decrees and circulars into operational inventory practices.

Sector-specific emission modules were developed to reflect accounting differences across major industrial sectors, and standardized reporting structures were implemented to enhance MRV readiness and reporting consistency.

**Impact:** Deployed at **six industrial facilities**, improving reporting accuracy and compliance preparedness.  
**Status:** Research manuscript (first author) **under review**.

---

### 3) Electricity Demand Forecasting & Energy Trend Analysis  
**Type:** Supporting Applied Research Project  
**Focus:** demand projection; driver analysis; planning inputs

This project examines Vietnam’s long-term electricity demand trajectories to support national energy planning and policy scenario development. Statistical and regression-based models were applied to analyze socio-economic and structural drivers shaping sectoral electricity demand.

**Outputs:** Generated empirically grounded demand projections and identified key drivers influencing demand structure, contributing complementary insights to broader national mitigation and transition modeling efforts (including international cooperation programs).
